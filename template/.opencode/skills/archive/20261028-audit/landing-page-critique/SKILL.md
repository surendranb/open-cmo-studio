# Landing Page Critique & CRO Audit

Systematic framework for auditing marketing websites, SaaS homepages, and product landing pages to identify conversion leaks, copy ambiguity, and messaging friction.

## The 5-Second Comprehension Test

Within 5 seconds of landing on the page above the fold, a first-time visitor must clearly answer four questions without scrolling:
1. **What is it?** (The clear product category and function)
2. **Who is it for?** (The target practitioner or economic buyer)
3. **Why should I care?** (The acute problem solved or primary business outcome)
4. **What do I do next?** (The single, unambiguous primary call-to-action)

If any of these 4 is vague, poetic, or buried, the page fails the 5-second test.

## Audit Dimensions & Scoring Rubric

Evaluate the page across 5 core dimensions (Score 1-5 each, total /25):

### 1. Headline & Above-the-Fold Architecture
- **Clarity over Cleverness**: Does the headline describe the actual product, or is it vague corporate aspirational poetry (e.g. *"Empowering the Future of Intelligent Collaboration"* vs *"Autonomous Marketing Agent Fleet for macOS"*).
- **Subheadline**: Does it explain *how* the product works and the primary mechanism?
- **Visual Anchor**: Does the hero section show the real product UI/screenshot, or generic stock 3D renders?

### 2. Call-to-Action (CTA) Hierarchy & Friction
- **Primary CTA**: Is there ONE clear primary action button with high visual contrast?
- **Action-Oriented Copy**: Does the button say what happens next (e.g. *"Download for Mac"*, *"Start Free Trial"*, *"View Live Demo"*) rather than passive text (*"Submit"*, *"Click Here"*)?
- **Friction Check**: Is an email form or credit card required before showing any value? Is there a low-friction secondary CTA for warm leads (*"Watch 2-min Walkthrough"* or *"Read Docs"*)?

### 3. Social Proof & Authority Signals
- **Logo Ribbon**: Are there recognizable customer logos or ecosystem partners?
- **Quantified Testimonials**: Do quotes cite specific measurable impact (+40% speed, $12k saved) with full names, titles, and company logos, or are they anonymous praise?
- **Third-Party Validation**: G2 badges, ProductHunt rank, GitHub stars, SOC2/ISO badges, press citations.

### 4. Feature & Benefit Mapping
- Does the page organize features around **customer outcomes** rather than engineering specs?
- Are interactive product demos, carousels, or video walkthroughs provided?
- Can visitors skim the page and absorb the core narrative via headers alone?

### 5. Objection Handling & Risk Reversal
- **FAQ Section**: Does it directly address the top 3-5 sales objections (pricing, data privacy, migration complexity, compatibility)?
- **Risk Reversals**: Money-back guarantee, free trial with zero credit card required, open-source code auditability, easy data export.
